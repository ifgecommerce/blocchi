Privacy

▥

This notice covers Quastri for iPhone and iPad, published by Francesco Guiducci, IFG eCommerce, Rome, Italy. Privacy contact: info@ifgecommerce.com.

On your device

The app stores games, statistics, progress and preferences on your device. No IFG account is required and this data is not sent to an IFG server. The app has no advertising, analytics SDKs, advertising tracking, AI services or cloud sync.

You can reset statistics and delete saved games in Settings, with separate confirmations. Uninstalling the app may remove local progress. We do not provide remote game-progress recovery.

System backups may include app data according to your Apple settings; this is separate from in-app sync.

Purchases and sharing

Apple services handle Pro purchases and restoration. The app checks StoreKit transactions to determine Pro access. IFG does not receive your payment card number or Apple account credentials. See Apple’s privacy policy for Apple’s processing.

If you share a game code, the system share sheet sends it to the recipient or service you choose. Sharing is optional.

When you contact us

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use them to respond to your request and address the issue. Avoid sending unnecessary data.

Support email is processed to handle service requests (GDPR Article6(1)(b)), for security and protection of rights based on legitimate interests (Article6(1)(f)), and to meet applicable legal duties (Article6(1)(c)). Retention depends on closing the request, documenting its handling and any legal duties or disputes; messages no longer needed should be deleted.

Email uses Google infrastructure and may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Google describes processing, subprocessors and transfers in its Cloud Data Processing Addendum.

Your rights

You may request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable by emailing the privacy contact. You may complain to the Italian Garante or your competent supervisory authority.
