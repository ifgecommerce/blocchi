Game guide

▥

Place shapes and fill rows or columns. Completed cells disappear together; other cells do not fall or move.

01

Classic

Place shapes and fill rows or columns. Completed cells disappear together; other cells do not fall or move.

02

Score

Each placed cell scores 1 point; each line scores 10. Multiple lines and consecutive clears earn bonuses.

03

Hint

Help is free. Assisted results are labeled separately, with no paid penalties.

04

Rotation

Shapes do not rotate in Classic. Rotation mode adds a dedicated control.

05

Daily challenge

The challenge depends on the date and rules version. No daily downloads are needed.

06

Games and progress

Progress is local and does not sync between devices. Uninstalling the app may delete it.

07

Lifetime Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase for this app. It does not unlock other IFG apps.

08

Support

Use Restore Purchases in Settings with the Apple Account used for purchase. Pending does not mean completed. Use Retry after a save error; do not uninstall the app if you want to keep local data.
